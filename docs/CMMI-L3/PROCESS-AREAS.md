# CMMI Level 3 Process Areas

## 1. Requirements Management (REQM)

### Purpose
Manage requirements and ensure alignment between project requirements and plans.

### Goals
- Manage requirements
- Ensure alignment with project plans

### Practices
- Obtain understanding of requirements
- Obtain commitment to requirements
- Manage requirements changes
- Maintain bidirectional traceability
- Identify inconsistencies between work products and requirements

## 2. Project Planning (PP)

### Purpose
Establish and maintain plans for project activities.

### Goals
- Establish estimates
- Develop a project plan
- Obtain commitment to the plan
- Maintain the plan

### Practices
- Estimate scope and effort
- Define life cycle processes
- Determine effort and schedule
- Identify risks
- Plan data management
- Plan resources
- Plan knowledge and skills
- Plan stakeholder involvement
- Plan the project

## 3. Project Monitoring and Control (PMC)

### Purpose
Provide visibility into actual progress compared to planned progress.

### Goals
- Monitor project against plan
- Manage corrective action to closure

### Practices
- Monitor project planning parameters
- Monitor commitments
- Monitor project risks
- Monitor data management
- Conduct progress reviews
- Analyze issues
- Manage corrective actions

## 4. Supplier Agreement Management (SAM)

### Purpose
Manage supplier agreements.

### Goals
- Evaluate and select suppliers
- Establish and maintain supplier agreements
- Execute supplier agreements

### Practices
- Analyze risk
- Evaluate and select suppliers
- Establish and maintain agreements
- Execute supplier agreements

## 5. Measurement and Analysis (MA)

### Purpose
Provide measurement results for managing the project and organization.

### Goals
- Align measurement and analysis activities
- Provide measurement results

### Practices
- Establish measurement objectives
- Specify measures
- Specify data collection and storage procedures
- Collect measurement data
- Analyze measurement data
- Store data and results
- Communicate results

## 6. Process and Product Quality Assurance (PPQA)

### Purpose
Provide staff and management with objective insight into processes and products.

### Goals
- Objectively evaluate processes and products
- Ensure non-compliance is addressed

### Practices
- Objectively evaluate processes
- Objectively evaluate work products
- Communicate and ensure resolution of non-compliance issues

## 7. Configuration Management (CM)

### Purpose
Establish and maintain the integrity of work products.

### Goals
- Establish baselines
- Track and control changes
- Establish integrity

### Practices
- Identify configuration items
- Establish a configuration management system
- Create or release baselines
- Track changes
- Control changes
- Establish integrity

## 8. Requirements Development (RD)

### Purpose
Produce and analyze customer, product, and product component requirements.

### Goals
- Develop user requirements
- Develop product requirements
- Analyze and validate requirements

### Practices
- Elicit needs
- Develop user requirements
- Develop product requirements
- Analyze requirements
- Analyze requirements to achieve balance
- Validate requirements

## 9. Technical Solution (TS)

### Purpose
Design, develop, and implement solutions.

### Goals
- Select product component solutions
- Develop the design
- Implement the design

### Practices
- Select product component solutions
- Develop the design
- Implement the design
- Prepare for product integration

## 10. Product Integration (PI)

### Purpose
Assemble product components and ensure integrated product functions properly.

### Goals
- Determine integration sequence
- Ensure interface compatibility
- Assemble product components
- Package and deliver the product

### Practices
- Determine integration sequence
- Establish product integration environment
- Ensure interface compatibility
- Manage interfaces
- Assemble product components
- Evaluate assembled product components
- Package the product

## 11. Verification (VER)

### Purpose
Ensure selected work products meet their specified requirements.

### Goals
- Prepare for verification
- Perform peer reviews
- Verify selected work products

### Practices
- Prepare for verification
- Perform peer reviews
- Verify selected work products
- Analyze verification results

## 12. Validation (VAL)

### Purpose
Demonstrate that a product or product component fulfills its intended use.

### Goals
- Prepare for validation
- Validate product or product component
- Validate product or product component for intended use

### Practices
- Select products for validation
- Establish validation environment
- Establish validation procedures and criteria
- Perform validation
- Analyze validation results

## 13. Organizational Process Focus (OPF)

### Purpose
Plan and implement organizational process improvement.

### Goals
- Determine process improvement opportunities
- Plan and implement process improvements

### Practices
- Determine process improvement opportunities
- Plan process improvement
- Implement process improvement
- Deploy organizational process assets

## 14. Organizational Process Definition (OPD)

### Purpose
Establish and maintain a usable set of organizational process assets.

### Goals
- Establish standard processes
- Establish organizational process assets

### Practices
- Establish standard processes
- Establish life cycle models
- Establish tailoring criteria and guidelines
- Establish organizational process assets
- Establish process improvement plans

## 15. Organizational Training (OT)

### Purpose
Develop people's skills and knowledge.

### Goals
- Establish an organizational training capability
- Provide necessary training

### Practices
- Establish strategic training needs
- Determine which training needs are the responsibility of the organization
- Establish an organizational training tactical plan
- Establish a training capability
- Deliver training
- Establish training records
- Assess training effectiveness

## 16. Integrated Project Management (IPM)

### Purpose
Establish and manage the project using a defined process.

### Goals
- Use the organization's set of standard processes
- Establish the project's defined process
- Tailor the organization's set of standard processes
- Manage the project using the project's defined process
- Contribute to the organizational process assets
- Contribute to the organizational training assets

### Practices
- Use organizational process assets and other assets
- Establish the project's defined process
- Establish the project's shared vision
- Structure the project according to the project's defined process
- Use the project's defined process
- Contribute to the organizational process assets

## 17. Risk Management (RSKM)

### Purpose
Identify and manage risks proactively.

### Goals
- Prepare for risk management
- Identify and analyze risks
- Mitigate risks

### Practices
- Prepare for risk management
- Identify and categorize risks
- Evaluate, categorize, and prioritize risks
- Develop risk mitigation plans
- Implement risk mitigation plans

## 18. Decision Analysis and Resolution (DAR)

### Purpose
Make decisions using a formal evaluation process.

### Goals
- Evaluate alternatives using established criteria

### Practices
- Establish guidelines for decision analysis
- Establish evaluation criteria
- Identify alternative solutions
- Select evaluation methods
- Evaluate alternatives
- Select solutions

## 19. Organizational Environment for Integration (OEI)

### Purpose
Provide a shared vision and environment for integrated project management.

### Goals
- Establish a shared vision for the project
- Establish an integrated project environment

### Practices
- Establish a shared vision for the project
- Establish an integrated project environment
- Manage the integrated project environment
