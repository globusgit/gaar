## CMMI Level 3 Overview
CMMI Level 3 (Defined) focuses on process standardization and documentation. The organization's processes are well-characterized and understood, and are described in standards, procedures, tools, and methods.

### Process Areas Covered
1. **Requirements Management (REQM)** - Managing requirements and ensuring alignment
2. **Project Planning (PP)** - Planning and tracking project activities
3. **Project Monitoring and Control (PMC)** - Monitoring project progress
4. **Supplier Agreement Management (SAM)** - Managing supplier agreements
5. **Measurement and Analysis (MA)** - Collecting and analyzing metrics
6. **Process and Product Quality Assurance (PPQA)** - Ensuring process adherence
7. **Configuration Management (CM)** - Managing configuration items
8. **Requirements Development (RD)** - Developing and analyzing requirements
9. **Technical Solution (TS)** - Designing and implementing solutions
10. **Product Integration (PI)** - Integrating components
11. **Verification (VER)** - Ensuring products meet requirements
12. **Validation (VAL)** - Ensuring products meet user needs
13. **Organizational Process Focus (OPF)** - Improving organizational processes
14. **Organizational Process Definition (OPD)** - Establishing standard processes
15. **Organizational Training (OT)** - Training personnel
16. **Integrated Project Management (IPM)** - Managing projects using defined processes
17. **Risk Management (RSKM)** - Identifying and managing risks
18. **Decision Analysis and Resolution (DAR)** - Making informed decisions
19. **Organizational Environment for Integration (OEI)** - Creating integration environment

## CMMI Level 3 Key Characteristics
1. **Process Standardization** - Organization-wide standard processes
2. **Process Documentation** - Documented procedures and guidelines
3. **Process Training** - Staff trained on organizational processes
4. **Process Compliance** - Processes are followed consistently
5. **Process Improvement** - Continuous improvement based on metrics

## Process Maturity Levels
- **Level 1:** Initial - Processes are unpredictable
- **Level 2:** Managed - Processes are planned and tracked
- **Level 3:** Defined - Processes are well-documented and standardized ✓
- **Level 4:** Quantitatively Managed - Processes are measured and controlled
- **Level 5:** Optimizing - Continuous process improvement

## Current Status: Level 3 Achieved
The GAAR ERP System follows CMMI Level 3 practices with:
- Documented development processes
- Standardized testing procedures
- Configuration management
- Quality assurance processes
